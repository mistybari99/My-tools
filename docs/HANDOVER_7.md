## NOT BUILT in 127-129 (said plainly)
- Screens are not registered (docs/regs/dealer127.md); no POS / counter screen for dealer sales; no bulk item import / variants UI for thousands of items (phases 124-126 belong to other agents).
- Customers and suppliers are still ONE master list for the whole database (master-data scope is a later stage): a party can be linked from two businesses. Cheques, schemes, tint data and every report are strictly per business; party balances are NOT shown in the cheque reports for that reason.
- Scheme accruals, claims and receipts are a register only: nothing is posted to the books (the accountant decides the entries). Sale returns are not netted in a sale-based scheme. Bounce charges are recorded on the cheque only (not billed to the customer's ledger).
- Cheque clear for a customer uses `sale_payments` (mode bank); reports that list payments by a fixed mode list (cash / mobile / card) will not show a `bank` column until they are extended.
- Tint jobs do not create a sale or an invoice; the amount charged is a number you enter (kept for margin only).
