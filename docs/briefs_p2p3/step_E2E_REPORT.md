## Not fixed - accepted / needs owner
- **U-1 accepted / needs owner.** `stk_opening` ("tracked items with no opening stock entered") cannot tell a new item with no stock from an item with stock nobody counted, so it is a reminder, not a verifiable condition. The way out today: enter an opening (0 is allowed) or a count. Whether `stk_opening` should leave the ready checks of `accounting_on` is the owner's decision; the phase81 row is not edited here. The e2e order-of-setup note (online switches need a sales channel first) is by design.
- **U-2 accepted (design lock).** `forms_post_to_ledger` makes the paper form the record of production / issue / count (`path_locked`, phase51); that is the purpose of the switch. Only documented here.
- **U-3 accepted / needs owner.** `packaging_consume_on` is one global choice by design; a box as a recipe LINE works for the mixed case.
- **U-4 accepted / needs owner.** `record_sale2` ignores the line unit because the screen converts (pos.js); an API-only caller must send base units. Changing the money path of `record_sale2` is outside a safe fix here.
- **U-5 accepted.** see above.
- **U-6 accepted, not a defect.** `recipe_book` is the personal book of one artisan: without `p_artisan` and without an artisan record of the caller it raises not allowed; the owner passes `p_artisan`.
- **U-7 accepted.** `packaging_return_mode = wastage` keeping the lost box in COGS is the meaning of the option; the advice text is not rewritten (rule: never rewrite existing text).
- Two conflict lists (`permission_recommendations` vs `grant_separation_report`) and "purchasing sees cost through the role seed" are observations of the run, not changed.
