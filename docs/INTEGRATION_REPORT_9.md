## 6. What could still leak (honest list)

1. `audit_log` is owner-only; nothing shows a business user the audit rows of org units. If an audit viewer is ever given to a non-owner, the rows of `org_units` / `business_profiles` / `legal_entities` (group rename 'MD-AL AMIN') must be filtered first.
2. The notify / backup bots (`_is_ops`) read group infrastructure by design (group digest, backups, schedulers): their messages go to the owner; a bot token in a business user's hands would see group data.
3. The `org_units.code` unique index is global; a non-owner's codes are namespaced by his business code, so a clash only happens inside his own business, but two businesses whose codes are prefixes of each other (HW and HW-X) could answer `code_taken` for a namespaced code. Choose business codes that are not prefixes of one another.
4. Data-bearing text typed by people (customer notes, expense heads, report brand names, location names) is not scanned for the words 'MD-AL AMIN'; the sweep uses seeded data. A person who writes the group name into a note shows it to whoever may read the note.
5. The sweep covers function answers and table rows with the seeded data; a function that only leaks under data the seed does not have (for example a business with a bank statement) is not proven. New functions: add them to `scope_gaps133` review and rerun `leak134`.
6. Offline cache (`coldstart.js`) keeps the last `my_context` on the phone; it holds no business names (location ids only) but the cached profile / brand is the business of that phone's user, so it is not a leak between users.
7. The web `ENV.locs` (location names of other businesses) is filtered by the restrictive policies only while `org_scope_on` is ON; with it OFF every user of a database with two businesses sees both businesses' place names (the walls are the switch). Group name and legal entities are hidden in both states.
8. Not verified on a real phone or on the owner's live Supabase; the logo upload goes through the existing photo helper and the photos bucket policies (mock bucket in tests).
